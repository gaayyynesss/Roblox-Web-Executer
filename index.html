Good to know

Questions

Is this really Roblox?

Yes. The Play button opens the official Roblox app running on now.gg's cloud servers and streamed to your browser. It's the real game with your real account — not a copy or a clone.

Do I type my password here?

No. This page only sends you to the cloud player. You sign in on Roblox's own login screen inside it. Never enter your Roblox password on any site that isn't Roblox or a trusted launcher like now.gg.

Is it free?

Yes to start. now.gg offers free cloud play with some waiting or time limits, and paid options for longer sessions. Roblox itself is free as always.

Will it work on my school Chromebook?

Usually, as long as the network doesn't block now.gg. Since the game runs in the cloud and only video comes to your device, it works on low-powered machines. If your school blocks the site, that's their network setting, not this page.

Is this an executor or a way to get free Robux?

No, and avoid anything that claims to be. Script executors break Roblox's rules and get accounts banned, and "free Robux" sites are scams that steal accounts. This is simply a shortcut to playing the normal game in a browser.

It's slow or laggy — why?

Cloud streaming depends on your internet. A strong Wi-Fi connection helps. During busy times now.gg may have a short queue for free players.
