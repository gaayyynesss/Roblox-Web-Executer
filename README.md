# ▶ Play Roblox in Your Browser — Free

Play Roblox right in your browser. **No download. No install. Totally free to start.**
Works on iPad, iPhone, Chromebook, PC and Android — if it has a browser, it can play.

### [👉 PLAY NOW](https://now.gg/apps/roblox-corporation/5349/roblox.html)

## How it works
Roblox runs in the cloud, **powered by now.gg's servers**, and streams straight to
your browser tab. One tap opens it, you sign in on **Roblox's own login screen**,
and you can jump into any Roblox game — just like the real app, because it *is*
the real app running in the cloud.

## Why people like it
- ⚡ **Instant** — no waiting on a download or install.
- 💻 **Any device** — even a low-powered school Chromebook.
- 🎮 **Your real account** — your games, friends and avatar are all there.
- 🔒 **Your password stays private** — you only ever type it on Roblox's own screen, never on this page.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages)
1. Upload every file in this folder to your repository:
   `index.html`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`,
   `manifest.webmanifest`, `README.md`.
2. Open **Settings → Pages** → Source **Deploy from a branch** → branch **main**,
   folder **/ (root)** → **Save**.
3. After ~1 minute it's live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo>/`.
