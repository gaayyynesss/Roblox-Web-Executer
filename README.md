# ▶ Play Roblox in Your Browser — Free

Play Roblox right in your browser. **No download. No install. Free to start.**
Works on iPad, iPhone, Chromebook, PC and Android — if it has a browser, it can play.

### [👉 PLAY NOW](https://now.gg/apps/roblox-corporation/5349/roblox.html)

## How it works
Roblox runs in the cloud, **powered by now.gg's servers**, and streams straight to
your browser tab. One tap opens it, you sign in on **Roblox's own login screen**,
and you can jump into any Roblox game — just like the real app, because it *is*
the real app running in the cloud.

## Add it to your Home Screen (like an app)
- **iPhone / iPad:** open the site in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
- **Android / Chromebook:** open it in **Chrome**, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- **PC / Mac:** in Chrome or Edge, click the **install** icon in the address bar.

Tapping the icon opens the site full-screen — then one more tap plays Roblox.
